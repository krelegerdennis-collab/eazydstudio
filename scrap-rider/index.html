Scrap Rider

A physics trials bike game. Lean, wheelie and jet-boost your way through a neon scrapyard, and beat your best time.

▶ Play now Join the Discord

Free in your browser, on phone and desktop. On a phone, play in landscape.

  • Real physics

    Your weight moves the bike. Lean back to clear a ledge, lean forward to stay on a steep climb.

  • Jet boost

    Fire the jet to cross what your wheels can't. Riding and flips refill the meter.

  • Flip it

    Spin in the air and land clean. Every full flip gives you more boost.

  • Chase the stars

    Earn up to three stars per level, and try to beat your own best time.

Three levels to start

Finish a level to unlock the next one.

  • 1. First Ride

    Learn the controls on rollers, a jump and a steep climb.

  • 2. Scrap Yard

    Logs, ledges and a big drop. Every obstacle needs lean.

  • 3. Neon Gap

    Gaps too wide to jump. Save your boost, or fall.
