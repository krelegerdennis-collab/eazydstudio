Hoshimi Haven

A cozy tower defense game: collect anime star-pets and defend your floating star village from the Gloomies.

Play in your browser Watch the trailer Join the Discord for updates

Early playtest build · best on a phone held sideways, or a desktop browser · about 40 MB, loads once and is then cached

Watch the trailer

  • Collect star-pets

    Befriend a growing cast of cute anime star-pets, each with its own way of keeping the village safe.

  • Defend the village

    Place your pets, plan your defenses and keep the floating star village glowing, wave after wave.

  • Pets never die

    No losses, no stress. A tired pet simply rests and comes back, ready for the next wave.

  • Never pay-to-win

    Nothing in Hoshimi Haven can be bought to win. Progress comes from playing, not paying.
