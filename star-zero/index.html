Star Zero

A 3D action game that runs in your browser. Chain your strikes into a finisher, roll out of the way, charge a heavy blow, and level up as you go.

▶ Play in your browser Join the Discord

Nothing to install. Plays with a keyboard, a gamepad or a touchscreen. On a phone, hold it sideways. The first load is about 20 MB. The game is in English and has no sound yet.

This is an early version

  • The art is placeholder. The hero is a test model, and the trainers and opponents are plain shapes. Menus and buttons are bare text.
  • It is short. There is one island, one courtyard called the Simulation and one test room. You get three drills, a final test and three combat rounds, plus a few opponents and a mini-boss in the test room.
  • Your save can be wiped. Progress is stored in your browser only, and a later version may not be able to read it.
  • There is no sound.
  • Expect rough edges. It has not been tested on many phones or browsers.

  • Chains and finishers

    String light attacks together and end the chain with a finisher. Lock on to keep one opponent in front of you.

  • Dodge

    Opponents show an attack before it lands. Roll out of the way, then strike back.

  • Heavy blows

    Hold the attack button to charge a heavy blow. Hit hard enough and an opponent staggers.

  • Levels and stat points

    Opponents and rounds give EXP. Every level gives you three points to spend on Power, Vitality, Impact and Reflex, up to level 10. Taking the points back is free.

  • Techniques

    Levelling unlocks four techniques and up to three slots to equip them in: a longer chain, a finisher that hits all around you, and a strike out of a roll.

  • Bigger finishers

    The longer the chain, the bigger the finisher and the light effect that comes with it.

  • Combat rounds

    Three rounds with waves of opponents that hit back. Go down and the round is lost. You can replay every round.

  • Weapons

    You start with your fists, and a trainer hands you a training sword. A blade, a pair of knuckles and a lance lie in the test room, behind a temporary passage in the Simulation. Each of those three handles differently and gives its own stat bonus.
