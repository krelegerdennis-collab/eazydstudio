Orbit Dash

A one-tap space arcade game. Your ship circles a planet. Tap to let go, fly straight and catch the next orbit. Miss, and you drift off into space.

▶ Play now Join the Discord

Free in your browser, on phone and desktop. On a phone, play in portrait. The game is in English, with a Dutch option in the settings.

  • One tap

    The whole game is one button. Timing is everything: let go too early or too late and you miss the planet.

  • Perfect combos

    Catch the next orbit fast for a PERFECT. Chain them and your trail changes colour as the combo climbs.

  • The void

    A purple horizon rises from below and speeds up with every planet. Keep moving, or it swallows you.

  • Black holes and boosts

    Deep in a run, black holes bend your flight: slingshot past one for a bonus, or get pulled in. Grab a gold star to freeze the void for five seconds with double points.

  • Six ships

    Unlock new ships just by playing: planets reached, stars collected and medals earned. No shop, no payments.

  • Levels and perks

    Every point is XP. Level up to earn titles, collect stardust and spend it on perks that make the game a little more forgiving. Perks are off in the daily challenge, so the board stays fair.

  • Daily challenge

    A new world every day, the same for everyone. Play as often as you like and race for the online top 20.

Leap, dodge, repeat

Rounds last one to three minutes. Then you go again.

  • Planet to planet

    A dotted line shows your aim on the first planets. After that you are on your own.

  • It gets harder

    Smaller planets, moving asteroids and faster orbits. Earn bronze, silver and gold medals.

  • Pick your ship

    Six ships, each with its own trail. Every one is earned by playing.

  • Daily top 20

    You play under a made-up name such as "Fast Comet 42". Joining the board is your choice.
