Stardust Avenue

A top-down cosmic zombie shooter. A meteor turned the city into Hollows, but you are Starborn. Pick a fighter, gear up in the armory and take the neon streets back.

▶ Play now Join the Discord

Free in your browser, on desktop and phone. Your progress is saved in your browser. The game is in English.

  • Three maps, three modes

    Neon Avenue is a story mission with a boss. Crimson Docks is eight minutes of survival. In Aurora Heights you hunt and seal six rifts.

  • Four fighters

    Leo the all-rounder, Vega the fast one, Atlas the tank and Nyx with her turret and grenades. Unlock them with stardust you earn by playing.

  • Star cards

    Every level you pick one of three cards. Three cards from one constellation unlock its bonus, so every run builds differently.

  • An armory that grows

    Pulse Pistol, Comet SMG, Nova Shotgun and Rail Lance. Unlock them, upgrade them five times and choose which one you start with.

  • Dash, roar, gravity

    Star Dash through a horde, charge a Lion Roar that clears the street, or throw a Gravity Grenade that pulls everything into a black hole.

  • A city that fights back

    Spitters, bombers and rift walkers, plus meteor showers, blackouts, supply drops and the Stargazer waiting at the end.

Take the city back

Neon streets under a ringed planet, in low-poly 3D with a synthwave beat that grows with the horde.

  • Lion Roar

    Collect stardust to charge it, then clear the street in one blast.

  • Pick a star card

    Eighteen cards in five constellations. Three of a kind unlocks a bonus.

  • The Stargazer

    Get out of the red circle, dodge the orbs and bring it down.

  • Crimson Docks

    Survival: hold out for eight minutes while the waves grow.

  • Aurora Heights

    Rift Hunt: find and seal six rifts before the clock runs out.

  • The hub

    Spend stardust on fighters and weapon upgrades between runs.

  • Two jackets: Solar Gold and Void Weave

  • One extra star card reroll per run

You get a code to enter in the hub under Style. No loot boxes, and it does not make you hit harder: weapons, fighters and upgrades are always earned by playing.
