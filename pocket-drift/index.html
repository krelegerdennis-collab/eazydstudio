Pocket Drift

A retro top-down racer on short, twisty night tracks. Your car drives by itself: you steer, hold one button to drift, and let go for a boost.

▶ Play now Join the Discord

Free in your browser, on phone and desktop. On a phone, play in landscape. The game is in English, with a Dutch option on the title screen.

  • One-button drift

    Hold DRIFT in a corner and the tail slides out. Sparks charge from cyan to orange to pink.

  • Boost

    Let go after the sparks for a BOOST, SUPER BOOST or MEGA BOOST, with a neon trail behind you.

  • Race your ghost

    Your best race comes back as a ghost car. After every lap you see if you are ahead or behind.

  • Beat the gold ghost

    A golden car shows the gold time on every track. Pass it and the gold medal is yours.

  • Six tracks, four cars

    Medals open new tracks and new cars, from the Starter to the Nova. No shop, no payments.

Neon night tracks

A rooftop, a harbor, a desert ring, a neon tunnel, a rainy city and a mountain pass, each with its own lights and corners. Three laps per race.
